About Me

I catch usability and functional issues before they become expensive to fix — because I've tested software, not just designed it.

I'm Gwen — a BS Information Technology graduate from Mindoro State University who works across the whole product pipeline instead of handing off at the wireframe. My focus is UI/UX design, but because I've also built front ends and tested finished software for a living company, I think about interfaces the way a developer and a QA analyst would: what's feasible to build, and what will actually break under real use.

  • UI/UX Designer — user flows, wireframes, interactive prototypes and design systems.
  • Front-End Developer — responsive builds in HTML/CSS/JS, Node.js, Vue, and PHP.
  • QA Analyst — manual test case design, bug reporting, and technical documentation.

How I work

My Design & Development Process

A structured approach that carries an idea from research through to a tested, working product.

1 · Discover 2 · Flowchart 3 · Build 4 · Test
1
Discover & Define
Understanding the problem — gathering requirements, identifying user needs, and uncovering pain points before designing anything.
2
Flowchart & Prototype
Translating insights into flowcharts and interactive prototypes to explore layout, flow, and structure.
3
Design & Build
Crafting polished UI and design systems, then developing the front end to bring the design to life with clean, responsive code.
4
Test & Refine
Manually testing features, logging bugs, and refining usability and visual details before final delivery.
