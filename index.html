I catch usability and functional issues before they become expensive to fix — because I've tested software, not just designed it.

I'm Gwen — a BS Information Technology graduate from Mindoro State University who designs digital products from the inside out. My focus is UI/UX and graphic design: wireframes, prototypes, and design systems that hold together under real use.

Because I've also built and tested what I design, I think about interfaces the way a developer and a QA analyst would — what's feasible to build, and what will actually break. That mix of design sense and technical grounding is what I bring to every project.

Outside of design work, I'm organized, detail-driven, and comfortable managing documentation, content, and communication — which is why I'm also open to virtual assistance and social media content & management roles alongside design and development work.

  • Experienced with PHP, Node.js, MySQL, HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and Bootstrap for building web applications.
  • Entry-level QA Tester — manual testing, bug reporting, software validation, and technical documentation.
  • Currently expanding into React, Next.js, and modern front-end & QA practices.
  • Open to full-time opportunities, freelance projects, and collaborations.
